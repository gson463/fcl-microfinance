\section*{LC-2 · Description of delivered software}

The Supplier has developed and is delivering to the Client the following software (the \textbf{``Software''}).

\noindent\textbf{Working title:} Microfinance Management System

\LC{LC-2.0} --- \textbf{Summary.} A single system for microfinance operations spanning branches, loan officers, loans, repayments, arrears, reporting, and governance of users and products. What each user depends on their \textbf{role} (Administrator, Branch Manager, or Loan Officer) and on \textbf{organisation settings}.

\LC{LC-2.0} --- \textbf{Scope note.} The list below describes the \textbf{functional scope} implemented in this delivery. Commercial terms, service levels, and any items outside this list remain subject to the \textbf{statement of work or main contract} referenced in Section 5.

\subsection*{LC-2.1 · Access, profile, and organisation branding}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \LC{LC-2.1.01} --- Secure sign-in; access controlled by user role.
  \item \LC{LC-2.1.02} --- Configurable organisation \textbf{name} and \textbf{logo} for the application.
  \item \LC{LC-2.1.03} --- \textbf{User profile:} name, email, phone, password change; \textbf{profile photo} where enabled for the role.
  \item \LC{LC-2.1.04} --- Display preferences (for example light/dark \textbf{theme} and sidebar styling).
  \item \LC{LC-2.1.05} --- Flow for \textbf{initial administrator account} registration where enabled (\texttt{/admin-signup} or equivalent).
  \item \LC{LC-2.1.06} --- Login page: support contacts, Supplier and partner links, app version, clock and network status.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{LC-2.2 · Administrator features}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \LC{LC-2.2.01} --- \textbf{Dashboard} with portfolio and operations indicators (including active and defaulted portfolio, disbursements, interest, collections, outstanding and default amounts, expected collections, daily activity, field wallet position, borrower counts, loans nearing completion, and related drill-down lists).
  \item \LC{LC-2.2.02} --- Dashboard \textbf{filters} by date range, branch, and loan officer where applicable.
  \item \LC{LC-2.2.03} --- \textbf{Branches:} create and maintain branch records.
  \item \LC{LC-2.2.04} --- \textbf{Users:} create and manage staff accounts and roles.
  \item \LC{LC-2.2.05} --- \textbf{Borrowers:} search and view borrower records across the organisation.
  \item \LC{LC-2.2.06} --- \textbf{Loans and disbursements:} manage loans and disbursement-related actions in line with the product rules configured in the system.
  \item \LC{LC-2.2.07} --- \textbf{Officer transfer (reassignment):} move borrowers between loan officers (admin workflow).
  \item \LC{LC-2.2.08} --- \textbf{Loan products:} define and maintain loan products.
  \item \LC{LC-2.2.09} --- \textbf{History and audit:} archived deleted loans, archived deleted repayments, loan increase approval history, and activity preview; \textbf{full activity log} for oversight.
  \item \LC{LC-2.2.10} --- \textbf{Prepayments:} manage repayments recorded ahead of the normal schedule.
  \item \LC{LC-2.2.11} --- \textbf{Field wallet trace:} oversight of field wallet movements and balances.
  \item \LC{LC-2.2.12} --- \textbf{Arrears} and \textbf{defaulters:} portfolio views for follow-up.
  \item \LC{LC-2.2.13} --- \textbf{Holidays:} public and business holidays used in schedules and officer workflows.
  \item \LC{LC-2.2.14} --- \textbf{System settings:} organisation-wide configuration (currency, fees, branding, tagline, etc.).
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{LC-2.3 · Branch Manager features}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \LC{LC-2.3.01} --- \textbf{Dashboard} with branch-level KPIs and drill-downs, subject to visibility rules (for example some interest detail may be restricted).
  \item \LC{LC-2.3.02} --- \textbf{Loan officers:} manage loan officers assigned to the branch.
  \item \LC{LC-2.3.03} --- \textbf{Borrowers} and \textbf{loans and disbursements} for the branch.
  \item \LC{LC-2.3.04} --- \textbf{Loan requests:} review and action requests that require manager approval.
  \item \LC{LC-2.3.05} --- \textbf{Prepayments} for the branch.
  \item \LC{LC-2.3.06} --- \textbf{Arrears} and \textbf{defaulters} for the branch.
  \item \LC{LC-2.3.07} --- \textbf{Settings} relevant to the branch manager role.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{LC-2.4 · Loan Officer features}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \LC{LC-2.4.01} --- \textbf{Dashboard} with officer-scoped KPIs and drill-downs (portfolio, collections, registrations, and related metrics).
  \item \LC{LC-2.4.02} --- \textbf{Daily field cash (float):} on working days, confirmation of cash taken into the field before full use of the app; simplified flow on Sundays and configured public holidays.
  \item \LC{LC-2.4.03} --- \textbf{Field wallet \& cash flow:} daily wallet, repayments, disbursements, expenses, taken (float), withdraw-to-bank where applicable; CSV/Excel/PDF export (officer $\times$ centre).
  \item \LC{LC-2.4.04} --- \textbf{Centres and groups:} maintain centre and group structure used for borrowers and meetings.
  \item \LC{LC-2.4.05} --- \textbf{Borrowers:} register/edit; \textbf{bulk import}; duplicate checks; guarantor; CSV export of selection; prepared loan template; re-loan approval request for defaulted where applicable.
  \item \LC{LC-2.4.06} --- \textbf{Borrower details:} full borrower and loan view; PDF/Excel statement export where available.
  \item \LC{LC-2.4.07} --- \textbf{Loans and disbursements:} originate loans, schedules, and disbursements according to configured products.
  \item \LC{LC-2.4.08} --- \textbf{Requests:} submit and track requests (for example loan increases) that follow approval workflows.
  \item \LC{LC-2.4.09} --- \textbf{Group prepayments} and \textbf{prepayments} (scheduled and advance repayments).
  \item \LC{LC-2.4.10} --- \textbf{Expenses:} officer expense recording.
  \item \LC{LC-2.4.11} --- \textbf{Attendance:} centre meeting attendance.
  \item \LC{LC-2.4.12} --- \textbf{Arrears} and \textbf{defaulters} for the officer's portfolio.
  \item \LC{LC-2.4.13} --- \textbf{Transfer centre/group (same officer):} move a \textbf{group} borrower to another centre/group (single row).
  \item \LC{LC-2.4.14} --- \textbf{Bulk transfer centre/group:} move multiple selected group borrowers to one centre/group.
  \item \LC{LC-2.4.15} --- \textbf{Additional float (taken):} add more office cash the same day after first taken (Field wallet).
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{LC-2.5 · Reports, arrears, and defaulters (scope varies by role)}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \LC{LC-2.5.01} --- \textbf{Reports:} operational analytics with summary figures. \textbf{Administrators} see organisation-wide data; \textbf{branch managers} see their branch; \textbf{loan officers} see their own portfolio.
  \item \LC{LC-2.5.02} --- \textbf{Date presets} and \textbf{custom date ranges}; \textbf{filters} by branch, product, centre, group, and loan status, and by officer---only where the role allows.
  \item \LC{LC-2.5.03} --- \textbf{Charts} and \textbf{export} of report tables to spreadsheet format.
  \item \LC{LC-2.5.04} --- \textbf{Branch-level} performance comparison on Reports: \textbf{administrators} only. \textbf{Officer-level} performance: \textbf{administrators} and \textbf{branch managers}.
  \item \LC{LC-2.5.05} --- \textbf{Arrears management} and \textbf{defaulters management} in the navigation, with lists scoped to the user's role and branch rules.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{LC-2.6 · General behaviours}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \LC{LC-2.6.01} --- \textbf{Repayment schedules} and recording of repayments, including principal and interest split where the product rules require it.
  \item \LC{LC-2.6.02} --- \textbf{Printed and exportable} documents from relevant screens.
  \item \LC{LC-2.6.03} --- \textbf{Audit logging} of important actions for accountability.
  \item \LC{LC-2.6.04} --- \textbf{Role-based access} and \textbf{row-level security} aligned to branch and officer scope in the deployed backend.
  \item \LC{LC-2.6.05} --- \textbf{Data export} (CSV, Excel, PDF) where the screen provides an export control.
\end{itemize}

\noindent\LC{LC-2.6.06} --- Some labels, workflows, or metrics may depend on \textbf{configuration} and \textbf{data} in the live environment. If a feature is tied to a specific contract deliverable, cross-check against the main agreement.
